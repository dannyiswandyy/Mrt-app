How this works. Train positions are estimated from typical timetables: first and last trains, peak and off-peak frequencies, and average travel time between stations. This is the same schedule-based method journey planners use when live positions aren't published. Delays and disruptions are not reflected.

Tap any station for the next three trains in each direction. Peak hours are 7–9.30am and 5–8pm on weekdays. LRT loops show one lane for each direction round the loop.
