# Explore MRT Spotter

[← Documentation](README.md) · [Try the web app](https://dannyiswandyy.github.io/Mrt-app/)

MRT Spotter is a Singapore rail companion for enthusiasts and commuters. Availability of timetables, weather, camera streams and live service data depends on the packed build and external providers.

## 🗺️ Network map

- Singapore MRT/LRT network with station search, interchange codes, directional train arrows and line selection.
- **2D and 3D map views**, with building geometry and railway track alignment where source data is present.
- Station cards that can be opened from map points or supported station-name links.
- Weather and disruption overlays where supported.

> Map arrows and arrival times may be estimated from schedule and operating patterns. They are not an unconditional claim of measured live GPS tracking.

## 🔢 Line Board and train journeys

- View train service information, line direction, next stops and departure estimates.
- Examine **scheduled train slots/runs** and linked trips where the timetable provides them.
- See short workings, special destinations, turnbacks and operating-duty descriptions when they are represented.
- Duty labels include **FD, HD, AM, PM, OP and LN**; more than one label may apply.

A run number represents a timetable duty, not a particular physical trainset.

## 📋 Spotter Planner and sighting log

- Pick a line, station and date, then filter a time window.
- View scheduled passing times, direction, destination, slot and platform when available.
- Spot first and last trains, some special workings and return trips.
- Record a train as **Spotted** or **Rode**, and review your own sightings.
- A logged sighting may anchor the displayed train position for that service day. This is *user-supplied evidence*, not independent GPS confirmation.

## 🔁 Railway intelligence

- Linked scheduled train runs and trip sequences.
- Operating history and supporting timetable evidence.
- Trainset-related confidence based on logged sightings; no guaranteed physical trainset assignments.
- Disruption and rail intelligence that distinguish observations from estimates.

## 🧭 Plan a journey

- Search stations by name or code.
- Compare routes, transfers, scheduled departure information, travel durations and estimated fare where provided.
- View the route on the map.

## 📱 Everyday features

- Saved stations, next-train views and an **Android home-screen widget**.
- Trip reminders, selected-line disruption notifications and in-app Android update checks.
- Bus arrival tools, station information and rail model references.
- RailCam Singapore YouTube integration, subject to stream availability.
- Weather from NEA via data.gov.sg, and rail news/service data where available.
- An Ask tab with built-in rail answers and optional AI-assisted responses when configured.

## Android vs website

The public website shares the core public UI but does not reproduce Android-only widgets, trip alarms, notifications or APK updater behaviour. The website may rely on a data relay for disruption alerts, crowds and news; availability differs when that relay is unavailable.

**For accuracy and limitations**, read [Data sources](data-sources.md) and the [FAQ](faq.md). MRT Spotter is a fan project, not a replacement for official journey guidance.
